# Fifi name and design note

Reviewed: August 3, 2026

This is a preliminary product-name and visual-distinctiveness screen, not a legal opinion or a comprehensive trademark clearance.

## Practical conclusion

- A character name is not protected by U.S. copyright law by itself. The U.S. Copyright Office says names, titles, slogans, and short phrases are not copyrightable, although original character artwork and writing can be.
- **Fifi is a crowded character/toy name.** Existing public uses include Fifi the Firefly and other children’s characters. That makes “Fifi” a poor choice for a standalone product name or merchandising brand without professional clearance.
- This release therefore uses **Fifi only as a friendly nickname inside ColorQuest Kids**, not as the app title, publisher, icon label, or separate product brand.
- Fifi’s artwork was created for this release as an original abstract **Color Spark**: an indigo paint-drop/spark form with a cream face and coral, yellow, and turquoise accents. It deliberately avoids the recognizable dog, flower, pony, and firefly forms associated with other public Fifi characters.
- A general web search found no obvious exact public use of **“Fifi the Color Spark”** at review time. That is encouraging but is not proof of availability.

## Before a standalone Fifi launch

If Fifi will appear in merchandise, a separate app, books, paid media, or a title/logo treatment, commission a comprehensive clearance search. The USPTO recommends evaluating confusingly similar marks as well as identical ones, considering whether the goods/services are related, and searching federal records plus wider marketplace sources.
