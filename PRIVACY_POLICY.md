# ColorQuest Kids privacy policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026

ColorQuest Kids is designed for children and families. The app does not require an account, email address, phone number, full birthday, advertising identifier, or payment information.

## Information stored on the device

A parent or child may create a local profile containing a first name or nickname, age, and selected avatar. ColorQuest also stores activity progress, resume positions, voluntary **I liked this** lesson choices, drawing drafts, read-aloud preferences, and artwork created in the Drawing Studio. These choices are used only on the device to restore work and suggest related learning paths. This information is stored locally in browser or Android application storage on the family’s device. It is not transmitted to ColorQuest servers because ColorQuest does not operate an account or profile server.

Parents can edit or delete profiles and progress through Parent Corner. Saved artwork can be deleted from the private gallery. Removing the application or clearing its storage also removes locally stored profiles, progress, and artwork.

## Artwork export

Artwork stays on the device unless a parent chooses Download, Print, or Share. On Android, an exported picture is temporarily written to the application’s private cache and passed to the Android system share sheet. The parent chooses the receiving application or destination.

## Read-aloud

ColorQuest can pass lesson text to the text-to-speech service supplied by the device or browser. ColorQuest does not record audio, use the microphone, receive the spoken text on a ColorQuest server, or retain speech-service results. Available voices and whether speech is processed locally or by the platform provider depend on the selected device/browser voice. Families should consult their operating-system or browser provider's privacy information for that service.

## Internet access

Core activities work offline after the installable web app has cached its application shell or when they are used in the Android app. Discovery Lab uses illustrations created by ColorQuest's bundled scene generator. It does not perform live Wikipedia, Wikimedia, image-search, or other third-party requests.

Math and Science lessons may offer a small set of manually curated links to official learning resources such as NASA Space Place, NOAA Education, and PhET at the University of Colorado Boulder. The destination appears only after a grown-up check and opens in the device browser. Those external sites have their own privacy practices; a parent should supervise their use.

## Data we do not collect

ColorQuest Kids does not include advertising, analytics, behavioral tracking, social profiles, chat, location collection, camera access, microphone access, contact access, or broad photo/storage access. It does not sell or share children’s personal information.

## Parents and guardians

Parent Corner uses a randomized grown-up multiplication check before profile editing, artwork export, printing, or deletion. This is a practical child deterrent, not an identity-verification or security system. A parent or guardian should supervise device sharing, external learning links, and Android’s system share sheet.

## Contact

Parents can report a privacy concern through the project’s [GitHub issue page](https://github.com/eharinathkumar/colorquest-kids/issues). Do not include a child’s name, artwork, or other personal information in a public issue.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published with a new effective date. The Play Store listing must link to a stable hosted copy of this policy.
