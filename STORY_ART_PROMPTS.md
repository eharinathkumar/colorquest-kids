# ColorQuest Kids story art prompt record

The version 2.4 illustrations were generated as original, local app assets with the built-in image-generation workflow. They do not copy or name any existing children's character, book, studio, or living illustrator.

## Shared production prompt

- Use case: `illustration-story`
- Asset: a four-panel children's picture-book storyboard sheet for an offline educational app
- Medium: polished gouache and soft colored pencil, rounded shapes, expressive faces, warm paper texture
- Layout: exact 2×2 grid, equal square panels, plain white gutters, no objects crossing panel boundaries
- Continuity: preserve each story's character, clothing, props, palette, and setting across all four panels
- Constraints: simple age-appropriate composition; no text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, copyrighted characters, frightening expressions, or photorealism

## Story-specific panel prompts

1. **Pip's Hiccuping Hat:** Pip tries an enormous yellow hat; a hiccup pops it up beside Ladybug; it lands softly on Snail like a tent; Pip and Snail laugh beneath it.
2. **The Moon's Missing Sock:** Moon searches a cloud bedroom; stars point toward Earth; Giraffe wears the striped sock as a scarf; Moon gifts it and wears cloud slippers.
3. **Tiny Turtle's Fast Day:** Tuck challenges a quiet red ball; rides a leaf downhill; splashes safely beside Duck's rain boots; celebrates at a daisy finish while the ball remains still.
4. **The Banana Boots Parade:** Nia finds safe banana-shaped cardboard craft pieces; wears strapped cardboard pretend slippers steadily; leads an imaginary kitchen parade; returns real fruit to the bowl and puts on rain boots. A safety edit explicitly replaced the first draft's real bananas underfoot with cardboard crafts.
5. **The Very Polite Volcano:** Vee feels a tickly puff; warns picnic birds so they move back; releases magical petals and soft steam instead of lava; everyone laughs beneath a leaf umbrella.
6. **Dot and the Bubble Bus:** Dot blows an enormous bubble; imagines it as a bus with her dog driving; butterfly passengers bring leaf parcels; the bus lands and pops into sparkling drops.

The six storyboard sheets were visually reviewed, cropped into 24 standalone WebP pages, resized to 900×900, stripped of metadata, and bundled under `public/stories/`. The source sheets are not required by the app.
